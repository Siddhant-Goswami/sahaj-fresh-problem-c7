## 1. Headline

Revenue Rs 11.4 crore for the quarter, up 18 per cent on Q1. Both new hubs live and despatching. The Resolution 31/05 cost programme has landed ahead of target. Subscriber retention in the two new cities is below plan and is the main watch item for Q3.

**Commentary (A. Nadig).** The Tier 2 cancellation rate is three times the mature-city rate and it is climbing month on month. That is not an onboarding-cohort effect; a bad cohort decays and flattens. Exit reasons captured at the point of cancellation are dominated by product condition (68 per cent of Tier 2 cancellations cite quality, against 19 per cent in Bengaluru).

Quality's position, at item 4, is that the product condition issue is understood and the fix is in flight. Growth's position is that we are acquiring into a leaking bucket. **Agreed:** paid acquisition in both Tier 2 cities cut by 60 per cent from 1 August.

The telematics changeover was the single largest contributor and was negotiated inside the Board's per-contract ceiling. The Managing Director asked that the programme go to the Board at the Q3 meeting as a closed item and that the team be recognised.

## 4. Product condition at the Tier 2 hubs

Ms Iyer tabled QA/2024/MEMO/17 dated 21 May. Summary as presented: the flexible packaging film introduced in February does not hold seal integrity under Tier 2 ambient temperatures, the effect is thermally driven, and the corrective action is a reversion to the previous film gauge at NSK-1 and IND-1 supply.

Status: purchase order for 65 micron film placed 4 June, first Tier 2 despatch on reverted film expected week of 22 July. Cost impact Rs 0.34 per pouch on Tier 2 volume only, roughly Rs 5.7 lakh annualised, absorbed within the packaging line above.

The Managing Director asked how confident Quality was. Ms Iyer said the lab evidence was clear and that she expected the return rate to normalise within four weeks of the changeover. **Agreed:** re-baseline at the August operating review; if the Tier 2 return rate is not under 0.5 per cent by 31 August, reopen.
