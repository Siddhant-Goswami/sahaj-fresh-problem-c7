# SOP-CC-004: Cold Chain Integrity, Chilled Dairy and Fresh Produce

**Document owner:** Head of Cold Chain and Quality
**Classification:** Internal, controlled copy
**Version:** 4.0
**Effective from:** 8 January 2024
**Supersedes:** SOP-CC-004 v3.1 dated 17 May 2023
**Review cycle:** Annual, or on material change to fleet or instrumentation

---

## 1. Purpose

To define the temperature limits, monitoring obligations and escalation duties applicable to all chilled product from cold room dispatch to customer doorstep, across all Sahaj Fresh city operations.

## 2. Scope

2.1 Applies to all chilled SKUs in Category A (milk, curd, paneer, buttermilk) and Category B (cut fruit, leafy greens, sprouts).
2.2 Applies to all delivery vehicles, whether owned, leased or operated by a third-party fleet partner, and to all city hubs including hubs commissioned after the effective date of this version.
2.3 Does not apply to ambient grocery, packaged goods or Category C (eggs), governed by SOP-CC-006.

## 3. Definitions

3.1 **Chilled band.** The permitted temperature range for Category A and Category B product in transit: 2.0 degrees C to 8.0 degrees C inclusive.

3.2 **Excursion.** A recorded product-zone temperature **above 8.0 degrees C sustained for more than 20 continuous minutes.** A reading above 8.0 degrees C that is not sustained beyond 20 minutes is a *deviation* and is logged but not escalated.

3.3 **Product zone.** The interior air volume of the insulated compartment, measured at the sensor position specified in Annexure 1 of this SOP.

3.4 **Polling cycle.** One complete round of temperature acquisition and transmission by the vehicle telemetry unit, from sensor read to receipt at the monitoring dashboard.

3.5 **Detection.** The moment at which the monitoring dashboard raises a visible excursion state against a vehicle.

## 4. Instrumentation requirements

4.1 Every chilled vehicle shall carry a product-zone temperature sensor with a stated accuracy of plus or minus 0.5 degrees C or better.

4.2 Every chilled vehicle shall carry a telemetry unit capable of transmitting sensor readings to the monitoring dashboard without manual intervention.

4.3 Calibration shall be performed at intervals not exceeding six months and certificates retained for twenty-four months.

4.4 Manual probe readings shall be taken at loading and recorded on the trip sheet. Manual readings do not discharge the obligations at clause 6.

## 5. Loading and dispatch

5.1 Product shall not be loaded into a compartment whose recorded temperature is above 6.0 degrees C.

5.2 Pre-cooling shall commence not less than 30 minutes before loading.

5.3 The hub supervisor shall sign the trip sheet confirming 5.1 and 5.2. Trip sheets are retained for ninety days.

## 6. Monitoring and alerting

6.1 The monitoring dashboard shall display current product-zone temperature for every chilled vehicle on active route.

6.2 The dashboard shall evaluate every received reading against the chilled band at clause 3.1 and shall maintain a running duration for any vehicle in continuous breach.

**6.3 Where the duration at clause 6.2 exceeds the 20 minute threshold at clause 3.2, the dashboard shall raise an excursion alert to the city control desk within one polling cycle of that threshold being crossed.** Detection later than this is a compliance failure and shall be recorded as such against the trip.

6.4 The city control desk shall acknowledge an excursion alert within 10 minutes of detection and shall contact the rider immediately.

6.5 Excursion alerts shall be logged with: trip ID, vehicle ID, hub, time of first breach reading, time of detection, and time of acknowledgement.

## 7. Disposition of affected product

7.1 Category A product subject to a confirmed excursion shall not be delivered. The rider shall return the affected crates to the hub for disposal.
7.2 Category B product subject to a confirmed excursion shall be assessed by the hub supervisor against the visual criteria in Annexure 2.
7.3 Disposal shall be recorded against the trip in the wastage register.

## 8. Escalation

8.1 Two or more confirmed excursions on the same vehicle within seven days shall be escalated to the Head of Cold Chain and Quality.
8.2 Any excursion where detection exceeded the requirement at clause 6.3 shall be raised as an incident under SOP-QA-002.

## 9. Records

9.1 Excursion logs, thirty-six months. Calibration certificates, twenty-four months. Trip sheets, ninety days.
