### 4. Expansion programme: Nashik and Indore

The Managing Director presented the Tier 2 expansion case. Summary as tabled:

- Two new city hubs, Nashik (NSK-1) and Indore (IND-1), targeted for first deliveries in the second quarter.
- Capital and launch cost estimated at Rs 2.4 crore for the first twelve months, of which Rs 1.55 crore is fleet, cold room and hub fit-out, and the balance is customer acquisition.
- Existing city operations (Bengaluru, Pune) are contribution positive but the Board has previously declined further equity draw-down before the Series B.

Ms Kutty confirmed that the launch cannot be funded from the current facility without either a draw-down or an offsetting reduction in existing operating cost.

Mr Bhattacharya asked whether the expansion could be staged, one city then the other. The Managing Director's view, recorded at his request, was that a single-city launch would not establish enough density to justify a second cold room lease in the same financial year.

**RESOLVED (Resolution 31/04)** that the Board approves the Nashik and Indore launch programme at an outer limit of Rs 2.4 crore for the first twelve months, such approval being conditional on the funding mechanism at Resolution 31/05.

### 5. Infrastructure cost reduction

Following from item 4, and from the deferred discussion at the 30th meeting, the Board considered the Chief Financial Officer's note dated 9 February 2024 on recurring infrastructure spend.

Ms Kutty tabled the recurring line items: cold room leases, third-party fleet contracts, fleet telematics and tracking, hub power and backup, and the dispatch platform licence. Total annualised recurring infrastructure spend of Rs 6.3 crore.

Ms Rao observed that the telematics and tracking line looked heavy relative to comparable subscription grocery operations she had reviewed, and asked that it be examined specifically.

**RESOLVED (Resolution 31/05)** that the Board directs the management to achieve an annualised reduction of not less than **Rs 1.8 crore** in recurring infrastructure cost, such reduction to fund the programme approved at Resolution 31/04.

**RESOLVED FURTHER (Resolution 31/06)** that the Vice President, Operations be and is hereby authorised to negotiate, renegotiate and execute vendor contracts to give effect to Resolution 31/05, subject to a single-contract ceiling of Rs 75 lakh per annum and to reporting of all executed contracts at the next Board meeting.

Mr Bhattacharya asked that the savings case be brought back with a line-by-line comparison against the incumbent arrangements. Agreed.
